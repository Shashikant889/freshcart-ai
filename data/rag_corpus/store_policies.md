# Store Policies, Delivery SLAs & Customer Protection

## 1. Quick-Commerce 10-Minute Delivery Guarantee
* **Delivery Horizon:** Orders placed within designated dark store catchment radiuses (under 3.0 kilometers) are fulfilled with a target delivery time of 10 minutes.
* **Delivery Fee Structure:**
  - Orders exceeding **₹500** qualify for **Free Express Delivery** (₹0 delivery fee).
  - Orders under **₹500** incur a standard logistics charge of **₹49**.
* **Late Delivery Guarantee:** If delivery exceeds 15 minutes due to operational or weather delays, an automatic convenience credit of ₹50 is deposited into the customer's FreshWallet.

## 2. Payment Methods & Security
* **Supported Channels:** FreshWallet instant pay, Unified Payments Interface (UPI - Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm), credit/debit cards (Visa, Mastercard, RuPay), and Cash on Delivery (COD).
* **Security Screening:** All digital checkouts undergo automated real-time fraud scoring (evaluating spend velocity, item count, and anomaly patterns). Orders flagged as high-risk ($Z > 3\sigma$) require two-factor SMS OTP confirmation before dispatch.

## 3. Freshness Guarantee, Returns & Refunds
* **Perishable Freshness Promise:** 100% replacement or refund for fruits, vegetables, dairy, or bakery items if quality or freshness does not meet consumer expectations upon receipt.
* **Return Window:** 
  - Fresh produce & dairy: Claims must be reported within **24 hours** of delivery.
  - Packaged pantry staples & non-perishables: Return window is **48 hours** with intact product seal.
* **Refund Settlement:** Refunds to FreshWallet are processed immediately within 5 minutes. Bank account or card refunds settle within 2 to 4 business days depending on the issuing banking partner.
